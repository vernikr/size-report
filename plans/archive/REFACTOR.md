The input is the audit of 2026-09-14 (SPEC 8, DESIGN 4, CORRECTNESS 7, QUALITY 3). What stands here is
**cleaning only**: volume of code and speed. What is done per release step lives in `PLAN.md` §5, known
defects and workarounds in `BLOCKERS.md`; the items below cite them rather than restate them.

## 0. Boundaries

- **Behaviour and the shape of the output do not change.** Not the numbers, not the order of rows, not the
  cells of the artifact, not the fields of the contract.
- **Byte parity is obligatory after every item** — otherwise the item is not closed.
- **No new features inside the refactoring.** Wanted something new → `PLAN.md`, not here (the list is §7).
- **One item, one commit**; a rollback is a removed commit. An item that would need a behaviour change
  leaves the plan instead of being “fixed along the way”.
- A comment describes the code rather than the edit (as in the consumer project).

written, while §2–§7 carry the dates and the evidence. Rows stand in the order the items were written, so an
address may sit under a younger one (`R-0.6` under `R-0.7`), while no address is repeated: the two rows that

What the rows quote follows one rule: a **cause name, a printed refusal and a printed report line stay

them is citing the code. A failure message of a check is retold in English: it is evidence about the check,
